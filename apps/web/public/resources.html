Resources & Benefits

Help for the whole community.

FEED isn't only for emergencies. It's for anyone trying to stretch a paycheck, find childcare, lower a heating bill, go back to school, or get back on their feet. Here's everything you can find — and how FEED makes getting it simple, private, and free.

Why FEED is different

How FEED changes the experience.

Finding help used to mean running a gauntlet of phone trees, disconnected websites, and paper forms. FEED closes that gap — for everyone, not just people in crisis.

🗂️

One place, not ten websites

Before: ten tabs, four different agencies, three dead phone numbers.
With FEED: search every program and local resource in a single app — benefits, food pantries, clinics, and neighbors all together.
✍️

The paperwork fills itself

Before: re-typing your name, address, and household size into form after form.
With FEED: your saved profile auto-fills applications. E-sign and submit from your phone. No printer required.
🏘️

Current because neighbors keep it current

Before: database last updated in 2019. Phone number disconnected. Hours changed.
With FEED: community-sourced information plus official data together, kept alive by the people who actually use these resources.
🔒

Private and free, always

Before: forms demanding sensitive information, no clarity on who sees it or why.
With FEED: end-to-end encrypted vault, no ads, no data sold. Free because it's community-owned — not a product.
The benefits

Everything FEED helps you find and apply for.

These aren't only for people in crisis. A working family, a student, a new parent, a senior on a fixed income — each category below has something for you.

Food & Nutrition

What it covers

Grocery assistance, supplemental food for infants and mothers, free community meals, fresh produce programs, and school meal support.

3SquaresVT / SNAP WIC CSFP TEFAP School Meals Meals on Wheels VeggieVanGo

Who it's for

Families with kids, pregnant women, seniors on fixed incomes, anyone who's between jobs, or anyone whose grocery bill has outrun their paycheck.

How FEED helps

  • Check SNAP eligibility in a guided 3-step wizard
  • Auto-fill the SNAP application from your saved profile
  • Find nearby food pantries and free meal sites on the live map
  • Track your application status in one place

Housing & Rent Assistance

What it covers

Emergency shelter, rent and mortgage assistance, transitional housing, home repair grants, HUD Section 8 vouchers, and tenant counseling.

Emergency Assistance HUD / Section 8 HUD-VASH Renter Rebate DAIL VHFA

Who it's for

Renters one bad month away from eviction, homeowners facing foreclosure, people experiencing homelessness, veterans, and anyone searching for affordable housing.

How FEED helps

  • Wizard narrows from "emergency shelter" to "Section 8 waitlist" to what fits your situation
  • Housing application auto-fill from vault profile
  • Map shows shelters and transitional housing nearby
  • Community pins flag available rooms and short-term options

Utilities & Home Energy

What it covers

Heating fuel assistance, electric bill relief, weatherization upgrades, heat pump rebates, phone and internet lifeline programs.

LIHEAP Efficiency VT GMP Assistance Lifeline

Who it's for

Anyone whose heating or electric bill spikes in winter. Renters and homeowners alike. LIHEAP income limits are higher than most people assume — many working families qualify.

How FEED helps

  • Utility assistance wizard surfaces all programs for your state
  • Autofill the LIHEAP application in seconds
  • Get alerted to program reopening dates via the feed

Healthcare & Mental Health

What it covers

Medicaid enrollment, free clinics, prescription assistance, dental care, mental health services, and Medicare savings for seniors.

Vermont Medicaid Dr. Dynasaur VPharm Medicare Savings CHCRR CCN

Who it's for

Uninsured adults, children on Dr. Dynasaur, seniors managing medication costs, anyone seeking mental health support or substance-use counseling — cost is not the barrier here.

How FEED helps

  • Healthcare wizard asks about coverage status and returns tailored programs
  • Autofill Medicaid enrollment from your saved profile
  • Map shows free clinics and mental health centers near you

Jobs & Income

What it covers

Job search support, resume coaching, paid skills training, unemployment insurance, career counseling, vocational rehabilitation, and youth employment programs.

Reach-Up / TANF DVR Hireability WIOA Training VT Adult Learning

Who it's for

People between jobs, career-changers, anyone whose skills need updating, people with disabilities seeking supported employment, and young adults entering the workforce.

How FEED helps

  • Jobs wizard filters by experience level, training type, and urgency
  • Unemployment application auto-fill from vault profile
  • Neighbors offer job leads and skill-shares on the community feed

Financial Help & Tax Credits

What it covers

Free tax filing, Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC), TANF cash assistance, renter rebate, and financial counseling.

EITC VITA (Free Tax Help) TANF Renter Rebate

Who it's for

Workers earning under ~$65,000 who may qualify for EITC refunds. Families needing emergency cash. Anyone who hasn't filed taxes because they couldn't afford tax prep.

How FEED helps

  • EITC eligibility wizard — quick income + filing status check
  • VITA free tax prep sites shown on your map
  • TANF application autofill and e-sign from your phone

Childcare & Family

What it covers

Subsidized childcare, Head Start / Early Head Start, Universal Pre-K, after-school programs, prenatal care, WIC for new mothers, and postpartum mental health support.

Head Start Early Head Start Universal Pre-K Prenatal Care WIC Afterschool

Who it's for

Parents of newborns, expectant mothers in any trimester, families with kids ages 0–12 who need before/after-school care, and anyone navigating early childhood programs for the first time.

How FEED helps

  • Prenatal & newborn care wizard matches stage of pregnancy to right programs
  • Childcare application autofill
  • Map shows Head Start sites and childcare centers

Legal Aid

What it covers

Free civil legal help for eviction defense, family law, immigration, record expungement, benefits appeals, consumer protection, and domestic violence protective orders.

VT Legal Aid Legal Services VT Disability Rights VT USCRI

Who it's for

Tenants facing eviction notices, parents in custody situations, immigrants navigating status questions, anyone denied benefits who wants to appeal, and survivors of domestic violence.

How FEED helps

  • Legal wizard asks your issue and urgency level, then returns the right provider
  • Intake forms autofill from your vault — faster appointment scheduling
  • Map shows legal aid office locations near you

Transportation

What it covers

Bus passes, Medicaid-covered medical rides, volunteer driver programs, donated and repaired vehicles, gas vouchers, and accessible transportation for people with disabilities.

Marble Valley Transit Medicaid Transport Good News Garage SVCOA Drivers

Who it's for

Anyone without a car, seniors who no longer drive, people with disabilities needing accessible rides, workers commuting to jobs without transit access.

How FEED helps

  • Transportation wizard matches trip frequency to the right program
  • Map shows transit stops and volunteer driver sign-up locations
  • Neighbors offer rides in the community feed

Disability Services

What it covers

Independent living support, vocational rehabilitation, assistive technology, Choices for Care long-term supports, disability rights advocacy, and benefits appeals.

VCIL DVR Choices for Care Disability Rights VT

Who it's for

Adults with physical, intellectual, or mental health disabilities navigating benefits, people who want to work with support, and anyone whose disability has been used to deny them assistance.

How FEED helps

  • Disability wizard surfaces independent-living vs. vocational options
  • Autofill disability benefit applications from vault
  • Connect with community advocates through the feed

Senior Services

What it covers

Meals on Wheels, senior companion programs, DAIL support services, Area Agency on Aging programs, in-home care coordination, and Medicare counseling.

SVCOA DAIL Senior Companion Meals on Wheels Medicare Savings

Who it's for

Adults 60 and older, caregivers supporting aging parents, anyone managing the Medicare maze, and seniors looking for connection as much as services.

How FEED helps

  • Finds senior services within your zip code
  • Simplifies Medicare Savings Program enrollment
  • Volunteer drivers and companions available on the community map

Veterans Services

What it covers

VA healthcare access, housing vouchers for veterans (HUD-VASH), state veterans benefits, peer support, and community-based outpatient care.

VA CBOC HUD-VASH VT Veterans Services VT Veterans Fund

Who it's for

Veterans of any era — from Vietnam to recent service — who need healthcare, housing support, or simply want to find other veterans in their community.

How FEED helps

  • Connect with peer support through the community feed

The bigger picture

A stronger safety net lifts everyone.

When more people use FEED — to find help AND to offer it — the whole community wins. Less duplicated effort. Resources found faster. Neighbors looking out for neighbors. This is what cost-effective community support actually looks like.

01

Put yourself on the map

Offer a skill, a ride, extra food from your garden, or just say you're available to help — and let neighbors know what you need too. Giving and asking are equally welcome here.

02

Flag safety hazards

See a broken streetlight, a road hazard, or a public health concern? Pin it on the map so the people who can fix it — and the neighbors who need to know — find out fast.

03

Start or sign a petition

When an issue affects your community — bus service cuts, housing policy, local clinic closures — FEED lets you organize a verified petition and gather real digital signatures.

04

Share in the feed

Post offers, requests, local news, and resource tips with the people around you. The community feed is how neighbors stay connected and keep each other informed.

Getting help should be normal, easy, private, and free — and helping your neighbor should be just as easy. FEED is built so both are true.
