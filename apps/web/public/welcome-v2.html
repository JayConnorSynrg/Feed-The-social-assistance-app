More than an app

This works because of you.

FEED. isn't a company trying to sell you something. It's a tool meant to be owned and run by the communities that use it — free forever, and never for sale.

The map fills in when neighbors add what they know. Resources stay current when people share them. The more of us who show up, the stronger it gets. FEED. is the system; the community is what makes it work.

The Bigger Picture

A stronger safety net lifts everyone.

When more people use FEED. — to find help AND to offer it — the whole community wins. Less duplicated effort. Resources found faster. Neighbors looking out for neighbors. This is what cost-effective community support actually looks like.

01

Put yourself on the map

Offer a skill, a ride, extra food from your garden, or just say you're available to help — and let neighbors know what you need too. Giving and asking are equally welcome here.

02

Flag safety hazards

See a broken streetlight, a road hazard, or a public health concern? Pin it on the map so the people who can fix it — and the neighbors who need to know — find out fast.

03

Start or sign a petition

When an issue affects your community — bus service cuts, housing policy, local clinic closures — FEED. lets you organize a verified petition and gather real digital signatures.

04

Share in the feed

Post offers, requests, local news, and resource tips with the people around you. The community feed is how neighbors stay connected and keep each other informed.

Getting help should be normal, easy, private, and free — and helping your neighbor should be just as easy. FEED. is built so both are true.

The Plan

Vermont first. Then every state.

FEED. is a Vermont nonprofit in formation. It runs here first — and the open system is built so people in other states can stand up their own community's FEED. The founder provides the system, not the infrastructure for everyone.

The vision is bringing FEED. to all 50 states with free community access points and kiosks — even ones 3D-printed in the shape of wheat or a sunflower — so finding help is as normal as having a library card.

Meet Sprout

Say hi to Sprout 🌱

Sprout started as a single seed in a community garden — and like anything that grows where neighbors look after it, Sprout just kept… sprouting. Part helpful sidekick, part very enthusiastic intern who actually read all the fine print, Sprout exists for one reason: nobody should have to face the maze of benefits and forms alone.


Sprout read the confusing government PDFs so you don't have to. Tell it what's going on — "I lost my job and rent's due," "my kid needs a checkup," "where can I get groceries this week" — and Sprout helps you find the right program, resource, or clinic, and even helps fill out the paperwork. A little nerdy about deadlines, weirdly excited about food pantries, and never, ever judges.

🔒 Private and secure by design

  • Sprout runs on enterprise AI infrastructure that's independently SOC 2 Type II–audited and HIPAA-eligible (Fireworks AI, audited since 2023), with privacy-gated fallbacks — and it never stores, keeps, or trains on your conversations (zero data retention).
  • Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest (TLS + AES-256).
  • Sprout only ever sees what you type and your city/state — never your vault, your documents, your income, or your exact location. Your files stay locked with a key only you hold.
  • FEED. applies HIPAA-aligned safeguards to your information and never sells it.

🩺 Talk to Sprout about your health, too

  • You can tell Sprout about a medical problem. It'll help you find the right clinic, mental-health support, or assistance program and point you toward care.
Sprout helps you find care — it does not diagnose, give medical advice, or replace a doctor. In a crisis it points you to 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline); in an emergency, call 911.

Add FEED. to your home screen

FEED. runs in your web browser — there's nothing to download from an app store. Add it to your home screen once and it opens like any other app. This works on any phone and any browser.

Open FEED. →

Open FEED., then follow the steps below in whatever browser you're using.

On your phone (any phone, any browser):

  1. Open FEED. in your browser using the button above.
  2. Open your browser's menu. Depending on your browser it's the Share icon (a box with an arrow pointing up ⬆️), or three dots (⋮), or three lines (☰) — usually at the top or bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen" (some browsers label it "Install app", "Add to Home", or "Install").
  4. Confirm — the FEED. icon appears on your home screen.

On a computer:

  • Look for an install icon in the address bar (often a small ⊕ or a monitor/download icon), or open your browser's menu and choose "Install" or "Create shortcut". No install option? Just bookmark it (Ctrl+D on Windows, Cmd+D on a Mac) so it's always one click away.

Don't see the option? Make sure you've opened FEED. directly in your phone's web browser (not inside another app), then try again.
